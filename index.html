Module 01

Video conferencing kya hota hai?

Simple words mein: internet/network ke through do ya zyada locations ke log live audio, video aur content share karke meeting karte hain.

🎥

Video

Camera logon ko capture karta hai. Camera quality ke saath framing, lighting, angle aur distance bhi matter karte hain.

🎙️

Audio

Microphone awaaz capture karta hai; speaker room mein remote participants ki awaaz play karta hai. Echo cancellation aur noise handling important hain.

🖥️

Content

Laptop screen, slides, spreadsheet ya whiteboard share karna. HDMI, USB-C, wireless sharing ya platform sharing use ho sakta hai.

Huddle / small roomChhoti team, short collaborative meetings.All-in-one video bar or USB webcam + laptop.

Training / town hallPresenter aur audience; sometimes multiple cameras and content sources.PTZ/AI cameras, audio DSP, ceiling/table mics, speakers, displays, switching/control.
Webinar / broadcastOne-to-many presentation; audience interaction may be limited.Computer, camera, mic/audio interface, streaming/webinar platform, optional lights.

What do these specifications actually mean?

  • 4K camera: detailed video capture. Lighting, distance aur framing bhi matter karte hain.
  • 120° viewing angle: wide capture — chhote room mein log camera ke paas baithte hain, isliye useful.
  • 95° viewing angle: narrower — medium room mein door baithe participants ko frame karne ke liye.
  • Dual camera on X72: wide + narrow view — bade spaces cover karne ke liye.
  • Mic pickup range: designed distance. Room echo, background noise aur participant volume real-world performance affect karte hain.

Module 03

Video conference ke liye kaunse devices lagte hain?

Har room mein har device zaroori nahi. Room size, use case, platform aur customer workflow ke hisaab se system banta hai.

Basic setup

  1. Display / TV: Remote participants aur shared content dikhata hai.
  2. Camera: Room ke participants ko capture karta hai.
  3. Microphone: Room ki awaaz capture karta hai.
  4. Speaker: Remote side ki awaaz sunata hai.
  5. Codec / compute: Meeting app run karta hai ya laptop ko room devices se connect karta hai.
  6. Network + meeting platform: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet ya approved service.

Professional room add-ons

  • Touch controller: Room se meeting join/control karna.
  • Expansion/table/ceiling mic: Door baithe participants ki voice pickup improve karne ke liye.
  • PTZ / AI camera: Pan-tilt-zoom ya automatic framing/tracking.
  • Audio DSP: Multiple microphones/speakers process aur manage karta hai.
  • Wireless presentation receiver: Laptop/mobile se screen share.
  • Mounts, cables, PoE/network switch: Installation aur power/connectivity ke liye.

Three common system architectures

All-in-one video barCamera + mic + speaker + compute ek device mein. Example: Poly Studio X Series. Simple setup.
USB / BYODUSB camera/bar laptop se connect hota hai; meeting app laptop par run hoti hai. Customer laptop central hota hai.
Modular room systemSeparate compute/codec, camera(s), mic(s), speakers, display(s) aur controller. Example: Poly Studio G62.

BYOD = Bring Your Own Device. Codec = video meeting ka dedicated processing/computing unit. DSP = Digital Signal Processor, audio ko manage karne wala device.

Module 05 • Crestron

Crestron AirMedia AM-3100 vs AM-3200

Important distinction: AirMedia is primarily a wireless presentation/collaboration receiver family. It is not the same thing as a dedicated room codec like Poly G62. Some AirMedia models also support wireless conferencing by connecting supported USB conferencing peripherals to a user's laptop.

Wireless presentation

Crestron AirMedia AM-3100-WF

📲

Designed to let users share from laptops, phones and tablets, with optional wireless conferencing workflow for supported peripherals and software.

  • Wireless AirMedia presentation; built-in Wi-Fi in the WF model.
  • HDMI output to room display, up to 4K output capability.
  • USB ports for supported conferencing peripherals.
  • AirMedia Canvas can show multiple sources in supported workflows.
  • Supports supported Teams and Zoom laptop conferencing workflows; check peripheral list.
Check the exact SKU: AM-3100-WF and AM-3100-WF-I have Wi-Fi. Don't assume every suffix/model has identical radio features.
Wireless presentation + room I/O

Crestron AirMedia AM-3200

🔌

AirMedia receiver for wireless presentation, with additional room connectivity and control interfaces useful in integrated AV rooms.

  • Wireless presentation over network; standard AM-3200 model is not the same Wi-Fi SKU as AM-3200-WF.
  • HDMI input and HDMI output; output supports up to 4K.
  • USB 2.0/3.0 host ports for supported conferencing peripherals.
  • RS-232 and IR/serial control interfaces for display/control integration.
  • Enterprise network/security and management features; verify project requirements.
Model suffix matters: AM-3200-WF adds Wi-Fi. Check local stock, regional model and current Crestron specifications.

The meeting app runs on the laptop. AirMedia gives the laptop access to supported room peripherals over the supported wireless conferencing workflow. This is different from a native room system running the meeting itself.

Which should I sell?

  • “People just want to share slides wirelessly”: evaluate AirMedia based on network and user workflow.
  • “They need camera + mic for Teams/Zoom from their own laptop”: verify wireless conferencing compatibility and supported USB peripherals.
  • “They want to join meetings without bringing a laptop”: assess a dedicated room system (e.g., an appropriate Poly Studio X or G62 configuration), not AirMedia alone.
  • “They need display control and more room I/O”: evaluate AM-3200 and the full control design.

Sales talk tracks you can use today

Customer se pehle requirements samjho, phir product recommend karo. Don't promise a feature before checking the exact configuration.

30-second G62 pitch

“Sir, agar aapke boardroom ya training room mein custom camera positions, multiple microphones ya existing audio system integrate karna hai, toh Poly Studio G62 evaluate kar sakte hain. Ye modular room system hai, isliye camera, audio aur control room ke hisaab se select karte hain. Main pehle room layout, platform aur existing equipment check karke complete compatible solution propose karunga.”

30-second AirMedia pitch

“Sir, agar users ko apne laptop ya mobile se room display par wireless presentation karni hai, toh Crestron AirMedia consider kar sakte hain. Selected models supported room camera/audio ko laptop conferencing workflow mein bhi connect kar sakte hain. Main aapka Wi-Fi/network, Teams/Zoom workflow aur peripheral compatibility verify karke correct model recommend karunga.”

Objection: “Humein sirf screen share karna hai.”

Response: “Understood. Agar video call room se join karna required nahi hai, toh full room video conferencing system ki zaroorat shayad na ho. Main confirm kar leta hoon: kya sirf laptop content display karna hai, ya camera aur room microphone bhi Teams/Zoom call mein use karne hain?”

Objection: “G62 mein sab kuch included hai?”

Response: “G62 modular system hai. Base unit aur complete room solution alag cheezein hain. Camera, microphone, speaker, display aur controller requirements room ke hisaab se final karenge. Main BOM mein included aur optional items clearly separate dikha dunga.”

Discovery questions checklist

  • Room dimensions, seating capacity and table layout?
  • Which platform: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet or BYOD laptop?
  • Do users join meetings without their laptop?
  • Do they need wireless sharing, video calls, or both?
  • How many displays and cameras?
  • Table microphones or ceiling microphones?
  • Existing speakers, amplifier, DSP or control system?
  • What network, Wi-Fi and security restrictions apply?
  • Need room scheduling or touch controller?
  • Budget, timeline, installation owner and support expectation?
